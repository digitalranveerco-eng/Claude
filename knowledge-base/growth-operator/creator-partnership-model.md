# The Creator Partnership Model

A growth operator supplies the monetization engine; the creator supplies audience
and authority. The partnership only works when both inputs are real — audience
without a backend is unmonetized; a backend without audience has no distribution.

## Partnership structures (pick by risk appetite)
- **Revenue share (most common):** operator takes 20–50% of net revenue from the
  offers they build and run. No upfront cash to the creator; fully performance-aligned.
- **50/50 joint venture:** a new entity (or clear written split) owns the offer.
  Used when both parties contribute heavily and want shared upside + shared IP.
- **Retainer + performance:** a base fee (covers operator's time/risk) plus a
  smaller % (10–20%). Used when the creator wants priority and predictable delivery.
- **Equity / profit-interest:** operator earns a standing % of the product line.
  Reserve for proven operators and long-horizon partnerships.

## Revenue-split mechanics
- Always split **net** (after refunds, processing fees, ad spend, platform fees),
  not gross. Define "net" explicitly in writing.
- Define the **attribution window**: which sales count toward the split (e.g., any
  sale of the JV offers for the duration of the agreement + a tail period).
- Set a **payout cadence** (e.g., monthly, 15 days after month close) and the
  **reconciliation source of truth** (Stripe export + the platform's ledger).

## Legal framework (engage a lawyer to paper this — this is structure, not advice)
A simple, enforceable partnership agreement covers:
1. **Scope** — exactly which offers/products the agreement governs.
2. **Split & definition of net** — the formula, with a worked example.
3. **Roles & deliverables** — who owns what (creator: appearances, audience access;
   operator: funnel, copy, tech, fulfillment ops).
4. **IP ownership** — who owns the offer, course content, and customer list on exit.
   Default: customer list jointly accessible; course IP owned by whoever created it.
5. **Term & termination** — notice period, what happens to in-flight cohorts, tail
   payments on customers acquired during the term.
6. **Exclusivity** — whether the operator can run competing offers in the same niche.
7. **Confidentiality & non-disparagement.**
8. **Dispute resolution** — governing law, mediation/arbitration clause.

## Outreach scripts for signing high-value creators
**Cold DM (problem-led, no pitch):**
"Hey {name} — your audience clearly trusts you on {topic}, but I notice you're
sending them to {free thing / nothing}. I build and run the paid backend for
creators in {niche} — offer, funnel, fulfillment — on a revenue share, so there's
no cost to you and we only win when you do. Recently helped {comparable} add
{specific result}. Worth a 15-min call to see if there's a fit?"

**Follow-up (proof + low friction):**
"No worries if timing's off. Here's a 3-minute Loom showing exactly how I'd
structure an offer for your audience — even if we never work together, it's yours."

**Qualifying questions on the call:**
- What's the most common question your audience asks you?
- Have you sold anything to them before? What happened?
- Are you willing to appear in a VSL and a few emails?
- What outcome would make this partnership a clear win for you in 90 days?
