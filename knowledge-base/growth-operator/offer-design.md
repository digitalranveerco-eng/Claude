# Offer Design: The Value Equation

Perceived value rises with (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement)
and falls with (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice). Engineer each lever:

- **Dream outcome** — name the specific status/result the buyer actually wants.
- **Likelihood** — proof, guarantees, and risk reversal raise belief.
- **Time delay** — show speed-to-first-win; compress the path to result #1.
- **Effort** — productize the hard parts (templates, SOPs, done-with-you).

## Pricing ladder
- Entry (£0–47): lead magnet / low-ticket tripwire to create buyers.
- Core (£500–2,000): the main transformation offer.
- Ascension (£3k–15k): done-with-you / mastermind for proven buyers.

## Guarantee framework
Tie the guarantee to an action, not a vague promise: "Complete the 4 build steps
and launch; if you don't land your first client in 60 days, we work with you free
until you do." Action-conditional guarantees raise conversion and filter tyre-kickers.

## Validation before build
Never build the product before the offer is validated. Validate with pre-sales or a
beta cohort. Build delivery only against committed buyers — committed meaning they
have paid or placed a deposit, not merely expressed interest.
