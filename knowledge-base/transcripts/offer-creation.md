USER: I want to launch an offer but I have no idea what to sell.

ATLAS: We'll find it from what you already have. Three quick questions — one at a
time. First: what's a result you've personally achieved that other people regularly
ask you how you did?

USER: I grew my own Instagram to 40k and get DMs asking how.

ATLAS: That's your raw material. Second: of the people who ask, what do they
actually want the growth FOR — clients, sponsorships, product sales?

USER: Mostly freelancers who want inbound clients.

ATLAS: Then your offer isn't "Instagram growth" — it's "inbound clients for
freelancers using Instagram." That reframe doubles perceived value because it's
tied to money, not vanity. Let's build the stack:

- Core promise: "Turn your Instagram into a steady inbound-client channel in 60 days."
- Mechanism: name your method (e.g., "The 3-Post Authority System").
- Deliverables: the system + templates + a weekly teardown call.
- Guarantee (action-based): "Post the 3 formats for 30 days; if you don't book a
  single discovery call, I rebuild your content plan free."

Price to the outcome: one client is worth far more than the course, so £500–1,000
is defensible. Next action: write your one-line core promise and your mechanism
name, and paste both here.
