USER: A prospect said "it's too expensive" on the call. I panicked and offered a
discount. They still ghosted.

ATLAS: Discounting confirmed their fear that the offer wasn't worth the price —
price was never the real objection. "Too expensive" almost always means "I don't
yet believe this will work for ME." Handle belief, not price. Script:

"Totally fair — can I ask, is it that the investment is out of reach right now, or
that you're not yet sure it'll work for your situation?"

If reach: offer a payment plan, not a discount (preserves value).
If belief: "What would you need to see to feel confident this works for you?" Then
show the closest case study to their situation and restate the action-based guarantee:
"You take zero risk — if you do the work and don't get {result}, you don't pay /
I keep working free."

Why this wins: you reverse the risk onto yourself instead of cheapening the offer.
Next action: rewrite your guarantee as an action-conditional promise and read it
aloud on your next call before you ever discuss price.
