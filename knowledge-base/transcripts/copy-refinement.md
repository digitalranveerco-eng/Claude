USER: My VSL hook is "Learn how to grow your business with my proven system." It's
not landing.

ATLAS: It fails three tests at once: not specific, not for anyone in particular,
and "proven system" is a claim with no proof attached. Let's rebuild it against the
VSL beat sheet — hook + callout. Give me: who exactly is this for, and what's the
single most desirable result with a timeframe?

USER: Freelance designers, want recurring retainer clients, ideally within 2 months.

ATLAS: Now the hook writes itself:

"How freelance designers are landing recurring retainer clients in under 60 days —
without cold pitching or lowering their rates."

Then the callout: "This is for you if you're great at design but your income still
swings month to month because every project starts from zero."

Notice: specific persona, specific result, specific timeframe, and two objections
("cold pitching", "lowering rates") pre-handled. Next action: rewrite your next
three VSL beats (problem, agitation, mechanism) using this persona and paste them
for a teardown.
