# Core Copywriting Formulas

## AIDA
- **Attention** — a specific, pattern-breaking hook aimed at one reader.
- **Interest** — agitate the problem with a vivid, concrete detail.
- **Desire** — present the mechanism + proof that makes the result believable.
- **Action** — one unambiguous CTA with a reason to act now.

## PAS (highest-converting short-form)
- **Problem** — state the pain in the reader's own words.
- **Agitate** — show the cost of leaving it unsolved (time, money, status).
- **Solution** — introduce the offer as the bridge, then CTA.

## The 4 U's for headlines
Useful · Urgent · Unique · Ultra-specific. A headline missing two of these is weak.

## Rules
- Write to one person. Replace "businesses" with "you".
- Specificity outsells superlatives: "11 booked calls in 9 days" > "amazing results".
- One idea per sentence. One promise per asset.
