# High-Converting Launch Assets

## 5-Day Email Launch Sequence (full template)
**Day 1 — Story + Problem.** Subject: "{specific result} (and the mistake that
nearly cost me it)". Open with a relatable origin moment → name the audience's
problem → tease the mechanism → soft CTA: "doors open {day}."
**Day 2 — Belief Shift / Mechanism.** Subject: "Why {common approach} keeps you
stuck". Explain why the old way fails and the new way works. CTA: "here's what's
inside."
**Day 3 — Proof.** Subject: "How {name} went from {before} to {after}". One case
study, concrete numbers, the exact steps they took. CTA: direct to sales page.
**Day 4 — Objections (FAQ).** Subject: "But will this work for me?" Handle the four:
price ("cost of staying stuck"), time ("designed for busy {persona}"), fit ("works
because {mechanism}"), risk (restate guarantee). CTA: sales page.
**Day 5 — Close / Urgency.** Subject: "Closing tonight". Real deadline (cohort cap
or price rise), recap the full offer stack, hard CTA. Optional second send at the
deadline hour: "last call — cart closes in 3 hours."

## High-Ticket VSL (full beat sheet)
1. **Hook (0–15s):** bold specific claim — "How {persona} get {result} in {time}
   without {pain}."
2. **Callout:** "This is for you if {3 specific situations}."
3. **Problem + agitation:** the painful status quo and its compounding cost.
4. **Credibility:** why the creator earned the right to teach this (results, story).
5. **Mechanism:** the named unique method — the "vehicle" that makes it work.
6. **Proof:** case studies, screenshots, specifics.
7. **Offer stack:** itemize each deliverable with a value anchor, sum the value,
   reveal the price (anchored far below the stack).
8. **Risk reversal + CTA + urgency:** action-based guarantee, one CTA, real deadline.
Pacing rule: deliver hook→problem inside 90 seconds or retention collapses.

## Landing Page Wireframe (copy slots, top to bottom)
1. **Above the fold:** result-driven headline (4 U's) · one-line subhead naming the
   mechanism · primary CTA button · proof strip (logos/numbers).
2. **Problem section:** 3 bullets agitating the status quo.
3. **Mechanism section:** "introducing {named method}" + 3 reasons it works.
4. **Offer stack:** deliverables with value anchors.
5. **Proof:** 3 testimonials with specific outcomes + 1 case study block.
6. **Bio/credibility:** who's behind it and why to trust them.
7. **Guarantee:** action-based risk reversal, visually boxed.
8. **FAQ:** the four core objections.
9. **Final CTA:** restate the promise + button + urgency.
Rule: one CTA, repeated — never compete CTAs against each other.
